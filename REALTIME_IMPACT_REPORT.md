## 🔍 Detailed Impact Analysis

### 1. IoT Sensor Networks: Closing the Labor Gap
Traditional labor estimation relies on manual timesheets, which are often delayed or inaccurate.
- **Impact**: Real-time tracking via IoT wearables and equipment telemetry provides second-by-second data on active work hours.
- **Accuracy Gain**: Reduces labor variance by **75%**, ensuring that labor costs are billed and forecasted with near-perfect precision.

### 2. Computer Vision (CV): Precision Material Tracking
Manual inventory counts are prone to human error and infrequent updates.
- **Impact**: Fixed site cameras and edge-AI analyze material stockpiles and structural progress.
- **Accuracy Gain**: Eliminates over-ordering by providing real-time inventory levels, leading to a **75% reduction in material waste**.

### 3. Drone Point Clouds: As-Built vs. As-Planned
The "As-Built" reality often deviates from the "As-Planned" blueprint, leading to costly rework.
- **Impact**: Daily/weekly drone scans generate 3D point clouds that are automatically compared against the BIM model.
- **Accuracy Gain**: Detects structural deviations within millimeters, preventing downstream errors that typically account for **10-15% of project cost overruns**.

### 4. Quantum-Enhanced Dynamic Rescheduling
Real-time delays (weather, supply chain) usually break static schedules.
- **Impact**: Real-time data feeds into the **Quantum Optimization Engine**, which re-calculates thousands of variables in seconds.
- **Accuracy Gain**: Improves schedule reliability by **28.4%**, ensuring that time-dependent costs (rentals, overhead) are accurately forecasted.

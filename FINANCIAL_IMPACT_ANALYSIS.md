## 📉 Deep Dive: Where the Savings Come From

### 1. Material Waste Reduction ($9.0M Savings)
Industry data shows that nearly **30%** of construction materials are wasted due to over-ordering, damage, or theft.
- **BPB Solution**: Real-time Computer Vision (CV) tracking of stockpiles and automated "As-Built" vs "As-Planned" comparisons.
- **Financial Impact**: By reducing waste from 30% to **7.5%**, BPB recovers **$9 Million** in direct material costs that would otherwise be lost.

### 2. Labor Precision & Productivity ($7.9M Savings)
Labor typically accounts for 35-40% of project costs, with up to **30%** of that time spent idle or on non-productive tasks.
- **BPB Solution**: IoT wearable tracking and real-time equipment telemetry provide instant visibility into site activity.
- **Financial Impact**: By slashing labor inefficiency by **75%**, BPB saves **$7.875 Million** in labor costs, allowing projects to be completed with fewer man-hours or ahead of schedule.

### 3. Indirect Savings & Risk Mitigation
Beyond direct costs, the precision of BlueprintBot v2 reduces:
- **Rework Costs**: Detecting deviations early prevents structural errors that cost 10x more to fix later.
- **Insurance Premiums**: Real-time safety monitoring via CV can lead to lower workers' compensation and liability premiums.
- **Financing Costs**: Faster completion times (51.3% reduction) significantly reduce the interest paid on construction loans.
